About me

Kevin Keithley

I am an analyst, builder, and operator drawn to problems that do not fit neatly inside one discipline.

How I work

My career has moved through economic modeling, data science, hands-on business operations, and structural integration. The settings are different, but the work has a common thread: understand the system, identify what is actually limiting it, and build something useful.

At the International Monetary Fund, I supported global economic analysis, built and maintained modeling workflows, and automated data processes. I later earned an M.S. in Data Science from Tufts University, deepening my work in statistics, machine learning, databases, and software development.

I have also built businesses and systems in the physical world. I run catering operations producing more than $250,000 in annual revenue, where I use software and process design to reduce friction and improve execution. As the owner of Body Transcendence, I developed an evidence-informed structural integration practice and worked with NHL player T.J. Oshie for approximately four years.

The projects I build now bring those parts of my background together: learning a system deeply, connecting ideas that usually remain separate, and turning that understanding into something other people can use.
